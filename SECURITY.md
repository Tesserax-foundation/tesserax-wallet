# Security

Report vulnerabilities privately: **Security → Report a vulnerability** on this repository. Do not
open a public issue.

Tesserax rewards them in TESS, from 50 to 10,000 TESS a report depending on its severity: reading
keys out of the wallet, its extension or its desktop application, signing what the user did not
see, or injecting script into its pages. The scope, the rewards and the rules are in the
[bug bounty](https://github.com/Tesserax-foundation/tesserax/blob/main/docs/bug-bounty.md).
